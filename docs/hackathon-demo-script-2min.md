# Hackathon Demo Script — Second Listen (2 minutes)

Project 8 is a fictional portfolio company created for this demonstration. No
real company or investment data is used.

This video combines a short real integration proof with the deterministic
fictional sample. The sample is intentionally labeled as illustrative; it is
not presented as a live AI result.

## 0:00–0:12 — The problem

**Screen:** Open the blank Second Listen workspace.

**Narration:**

> After an investment, important signals are often buried in casual conversations.
> Second Listen turns a spoken check-in or a recording into an evidence-backed
> investment note.

## 0:12–0:20 — The scenario

**Screen:** Enter `Project 8` in the company field.

**Narration:**

> This is Project 8, a fictional portfolio company created for this demo.
> I’ll show how an investor can capture what changed after an investment.

## 0:20–0:35 — Real voice path

**Screen:** Click `Start debrief` and speak naturally.

**Investor:**

> The balance sheet looks better than six months ago, but cash flow has not improved.

**Narration, after the agent responds:**

> In the live path, AssemblyAI transcribes the investor’s voice while the agent
> listens and asks a focused follow-up question.

**Screen:** Show the live transcript and the agent’s response. End the live
debrief before continuing.

## 0:35–1:35 — The complete walkthrough

**Screen:** Click `Watch a sample`.

**Narration:**

> Now let’s follow a complete fictional walkthrough. The sample is clearly
> labeled so you can focus on the product behavior.

Let the sample voices play without speaking over them:

**Investor:**

> The founder seemed upbeat. They have a new office, but our CFO left last month.
> Nobody has formally taken over finance.

**Partner:**

> Who is covering finance, and since when?

**Investor:**

> I need to check. They also moved some of the R&D grant to cover payroll. Revenue
> has been flat for two quarters.

**Partner:**

> Was there written approval to use the grant for payroll?

**Investor:**

> I’m not sure.

**Partner:**

> Shall we add a follow-up to request the written approval?

**Investor:**

> Yes. Assign it to Alex, due next Monday.

**Partner:**

> The follow-up is recorded for Alex, due next Monday.

## 1:35–1:52 — Why the report matters

**Screen:** Pause on the completed right-hand report. Show the risk signals,
source quotes, review flags, and action item.

**Narration:**

> Each risk signal appears only after its supporting statement has been spoken.
> The note keeps the original quote, marks items for review, and turns an agreed
> next step into a follow-up with an owner and deadline.

## 1:52–2:00 — The second input

**Screen:** Close the sample, show the recording upload path and the resulting
report or a short prepared screen capture of it.

**Narration:**

> The same review can also start from an uploaded recording. The input is
> different, but the output is consistent: previous follow-ups, risk signals,
> source quotes, and agreed actions.

**Final screen:** Second Listen logo or completed report.

**Closing line:**

> Second Listen helps investors remember what changed, verify what matters, and
> carry the right follow-up into the next check-in.

## Recording notes

- Keep the narration separate from the sample dialogue; do not talk over the
  two voices.
- Show the `FICTIONAL COMPANY · PROJECT 8` label at least once.
- If the real live path is unstable during recording, use a short real recording
  upload result as the integration proof and keep the full walkthrough in the
  labeled sample.
- Do not show API keys, local paths, browser developer tools, or real recordings.
